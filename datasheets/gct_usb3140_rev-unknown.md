## Why this citation

**Comparison-only reference, not a candidate part for this design** (this
design's connector-choice decision selected USB-C, not Micro-USB-B — see
`hardware/schematic/bench-imu-01-design.md` §2). Cited only to show that
the "tallest component" board-geometry answer would differ significantly
(a vertical Micro-USB-B receptacle at ~6.5–6.9mm is *taller* than a
horizontal USB-C receptacle at ~3.2mm, DS-CONN-003) had the connector
decision gone the other way — relevant context for the future Mechanical
Lead's enclosure Z-height budget.
