## Why this citation

Real, currently-orderable, bare/unshrouded 4-position, 2.54mm-pitch,
straight through-hole header — genuinely matches the plain
`Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm:PinHeader_1x04_P2.54mm_Vertical` KiCad
footprint already placed at J2/J3 on this board (a simple unshrouded pin
strip, no locking/friction-lock housing). **Deliberately not** a
Molex KK 254-series part (e.g. 22-27-2041) despite that family also being
a common "4-pin 2.54mm header" search result — KK 254 headers have an
integral friction-lock plastic shroud/housing around the pins, a
different physical footprint than the bare/unshrouded style this board's
own KiCad footprint represents; citing it would silently overclaim a
footprint match that doesn't actually hold. Covers J2 (UART header) and
J3 (SWD header) — same generic part, both positions.
