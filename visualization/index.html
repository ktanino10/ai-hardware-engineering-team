Interactive explanations of the public Bench-IMU-01 artifacts.

Explore the circuit, mechanical parts and project records. These viewers explain recorded artifacts; they are not proof of current physical fit, safe operation or Design Complete.

English documentation · How to read these viewers

Circuit & Current-Flow Viewer
English UI. Switch between power distribution, implemented open-loop bench behavior and the explicitly unimplemented closed-loop concept. Click a component or wire for its explanation and source. Animated pulses are explanatory, not measured current.
3D Assembly & Part Inspector
English UI. Orbit, zoom, switch assembled/exploded views, and inspect each part. Source-derived meshes and simplified purchased-part shapes use a presentation layout, not a full installed-state or collision check. This is not Fusion assembly-process evidence.
Project Dashboard
EN/JA UI. Fetches public main-branch requirements, findings and other records on load or Refresh. Source prose, IDs and governance terms remain unchanged in either language. Parsing, CDN caching and dated static settings have explicit limits; the dashboard is read-only, not an approval engine.

The circuit and assembly viewers use checked-in data; the dashboard fetches public records. Repository links leave this Pages site. No language edition changes source values, review verdicts or human gates.
